People v. Meta Platforms Inc. (2026-07-22)

日期: 2026-07-22

美国加州南区联邦法院7月22日作出历史性裁决，拒绝Meta Platforms Inc.（原Facebook）关于虚拟现实数据隐私诉讼的动议驳回申请，这标志着该案将进入证据发现阶段。该案源于Meta旗下的VR平台Horizon Worlds被指控在未经用户明示同意的情况下收集生物识别数据，包括眼球运动轨迹、手部姿态和空间定位信息。法律焦点集中在《生物识别信息隐私法》（BIPA）第15/20.60节的适用性争议上。原告律师指出："元宇宙环境下用户行为轨迹本质上属于生物识别数据的扩展领域，Meta的数据采集机制违反了用户自主权原则"[1]。Meta辩称："现有法律框架无法适用于虚拟环境中的非物理生物特征数据"[2]。此案引发全球关注，因为它涉及数字时代隐私权的边界重新定义。电子前沿基金会（EFF）评论："如果法院支持Meta的论点，将为科技公司开启隐私规避的先例"[3]。判决可能影响正在审议的《数字服务法案》中的相关条款，欧盟数字事务委员会主席在推特上表示："这个案例对全球数据治理具有示范意义"[4]。

Cardiovascular Risks of Extreme Intermittent Fasting

日期: 2026-07-21

A landmark study published in *Nature* (2026-07-21) has reignited debates about extreme intermittent fasting protocols. The research, involving 150,000 participants across 12 countries, found a statistically significant 23% increase in arrhythmia incidents among those practicing 5:2 fasting compared to moderate time-restricted eaters (8/16 schedule). Principal investigator Dr. Elena Torres emphasized: "Our findings challenge the 'one-size-fits-all' approach to fasting, particularly for individuals with pre-existing cardiovascular conditions" [1]. The study employed dual-energy X-ray absorptiometry (DXA) scans and 24/7 cardiac telemetry monitoring over 18 months. Critics, however, question the reliance on self-reported fasting adherence logs rather than continuous biomarker tracking. Dr. Raj Patel of Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health argued: "Without objective metabolic measurements, we cannot rule out confounding variables like caffeine intake or sleep deprivation affecting heart rhythm" [2]. The controversy has split the nutrition science community. Proponents of intermittent fasting cite the study's rigorous sample size and longitudinal design, while detractors highlight methodological gaps. The American Heart Association issued a statement calling for "cautious interpretation until replication studies are conducted" [3].
