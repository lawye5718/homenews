2026年5月27日，北京通州区启动河道清理工程，约500名居民上街抗议。冲突始于5月26日晚，居民发现挖掘机进入社区绿地，开始拆除树木和绿化带。居民代表李某表示：“我们不是反对环境整治，但突然动工没有通知，且清理范围超出公告内容，涉及社区唯一的健身广场和儿童乐园。”官方称行动旨在“改善水体流通，防止夏季水位上升”，但未提供环境影响报告。

On May 27, 2026, the California Supreme Court heard arguments in *People v. TechCorp*, a landmark case addressing algorithmic bias in hiring practices. TechCorp faces allegations of systematically discriminating against minority applicants through its proprietary algorithm.

The case centers on TechCorp's AI recruitment tool, which allegedly downgrades resumes from minority candidates by 40% compared to their non-minority counterparts with similar qualifications. Plaintiffs presented evidence showing the algorithm weights "cultural fit" metrics disproportionately, favoring candidates from majority demographics.

A Nature study (May 27, 2026) published in the journal *Nature Biotechnology* has ignited controversy after revealing that 12% of athletes who underwent CRISPR-mediated myostatin suppression developed unexpected cardiomyopathies within 18 months.

The study tracked 150 athletes who received CRISPR treatment to suppress myostatin, a protein that inhibits muscle growth. While the treatment successfully increased muscle mass by 25%, follow-up cardiac MRI scans showed that 18 participants developed dilated cardiomyopathy, a condition where the heart becomes enlarged and cannot pump blood effectively.
